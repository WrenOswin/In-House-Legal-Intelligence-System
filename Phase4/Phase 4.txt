4. OBSERVATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Test labels are recoverable from the released files (test.json label field; dev.json contains the test
   set) - not used. Validation is on the 994 clean dev docs.

2. Outcome evidence is spread over the whole judgment: TF-IDF whole doc 0.690 > last 512 words 0.640 > first
   512 words 0.593 -> chunked (hierarchical) document model over many chunks, not just the ending.

3. Sections (frozen-feature probe, all four architectures): the last chunk is the most informative single
   section (A/C/D: last beats first by +0.025 to +0.053; TF-IDF the same, +0.047), the opening chunk (case
   history, parties) the least. With frozen features, pooling more chunks does not help (except the weak B
   encoder, 0.561 -> 0.591), and mean / max / attention pooling differ by <= 0.02 (noise).

4. Fine-tuning is essential: frozen Phase-1 features reach only 0.55-0.62 (below TF-IDF 0.69), fine-tuned
   models 0.71-0.77. The Phase-1 LMs (next-token pretraining on cased modern text) transfer to CJPE only after
   adaptation of the backbone itself.

5. Prior shift: train 41% positive, val/test 50% -> class-balanced CE + a val-tuned threshold. The predicted
   positive rate swings between evals (e.g. J1 0.56 -> 0.64 -> 0.34 -> 0.46); the threshold (0.39-0.65)
   corrects it. At the selected checkpoints the gain over 0.5 is 0.000-0.010 (J1 +0.001, J4 +0.009, J5 0).

6. Learning rate matters for LoRMA here: lr 5e-4 drifted into a constant predictor within one epoch; lr 1e-4
   with 32 docs/step learned steadily.

7. Techniques (Codabench): KD student 0.771 > LoRMA (A d768) 0.743 > partial FT (D) 0.705 > adapters (C) 0.591
   > BitFit (B) 0.527. Methods that change more of the network or the representation inside each block (LoRMA's
   multiplicative update on every projection, partial FT of 4 whole blocks, full FT in the student) work;
   methods with very few or very local parameters (BitFit 0.04M biases, bottleneck adapters at lr 2e-4) stay
   close to the frozen-feature level. Architecture and method are confounded (one method per architecture).

8. Parameter-efficient vs full: LoRMA trains 1.4% of A d768's weights and reaches 0.743; the fully fine-tuned
   58M KD student reaches 0.771. Partial FT of D (33% of its weights) 0.705.

9. Knowledge distillation: the 58M student (full FT on teacher logits + labels) beats its 140M LoRMA teacher on
   val (0.7735 vs 0.7417) AND test (0.7706 vs 0.7431), with 43% of the training memory (1.36 vs 3.19 GB) and
   60% of the inference time. It also learns much faster (val 0.707 at step 495 vs the teacher's 0.589). Part of
   the gain may come from full fine-tuning the student (vs LoRMA in the teacher); the same student without the
   teacher was not trained, so the two effects are not separated.

10. Val predicts test well in Phase 4 (994 val docs vs 10 in Phase 3): J1 +0.001, J5 -0.003, ensemble -0.006,
    J2 -0.030, J4 -0.030, J3 -0.045. Val ranks all six submissions in the same order as Codabench; the weaker,
    still-learning models lose more on test (less stable thresholds).

11. Ensembling gives no test gain: J1+J4 0.7426 vs J1 alone 0.7431 (the +0.007 on val was within noise and
    partly selection); no ensemble with J5 beats J5 on val. Unlike Phases 2-3, one strong model is enough here.

12. BitFit on the B encoder (0.040M trainable bias parameters) barely learns CJPE: train loss stays at chance
    (0.687-0.697), val 0.557, test 0.527 - below even the frozen-B probe (0.56-0.59).

14. KD vs no KD (K1 = J5's student without a teacher): val 0.7625 vs 0.7735 (+0.011, within noise) at the end,
    but +0.09 at step 495 (0.618 vs 0.707). Distillation mainly speeds learning; most of the student-beats-teacher
    gap comes from full fine-tuning of the smaller A d560.
15. Method control on D: partial FT (top 4 blocks, 18.2M params) 0.7352 vs LoRMA (0.82M) 0.6357 - on D, LoRMA
    at lr 1e-4 is clearly weaker; on A d768 (J1) the same LoRMA recipe reached 0.742.
16. Full FT + KD rescues weak techniques: C 0.635 (adapters) -> 0.785 (full FT + KD, best single model);
    B 0.557 (BitFit) -> 0.615 (LoRMA + KD). With the same recipe, C (prefix-LM) > A d560 > B, so the prefix-LM
    architecture is a strong student for CJPE.
17. Ensembling two strong models of different architectures helps on test too: J5 + K3 0.7771 vs J5 0.7706 and
    K3 0.7586 (+0.007 over the best member), unlike J1 + J4 in round 1 (no gain).
18. K3 (C full FT + KD) had the best val (0.785) but lost 0.026 on test (0.759 < J5 0.771): with 994 val docs a
    0.01 val gap between strong models is not reliable. Round-2 test - val: K1 -0.001, K2 -0.007, K5 -0.019,
    K3 -0.026, K4 -0.038. KD baseline confirmed on test: K1 0.7618 vs J5 0.7706 (+0.009 from KD).
13. Cost (2026-10-17, throttled to ~60% GPU): B 42 min / 0.74 GB, D 67 min / 1.26 GB, C 77 min / 1.32 GB,
    A d560 KD 118 min / 1.36 GB (+31 min teacher logits), A d768 LoRMA 138 min / 3.19 GB. Total ~7.5 h of
    training for the six submissions.
